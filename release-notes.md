## Sanskrit Translator v1.1 (Android APK)

**Download:** https://github.com/veera08231/translater/releases/download/v1.1/sanskrit-translator.apk

Install over v1.0 - your history and settings are kept.

**What is new in v1.1**
- The camera now opens as soon as you tap the Scan tab (it asks for permission once).
- Tap the camera picture to open it full screen.
- Zoom in and out with - and +, and turn on the torch for dark places.
- If you ever block the camera by mistake, Scan now offers **Open Settings**.

**How to install**
1. Download the APK on your phone.
2. Tap it and allow installs from this source when Android asks.
3. Tap Install.

**Needs:** the translator server https://translater-5zmo.onrender.com - no API key, no account, no cost.
